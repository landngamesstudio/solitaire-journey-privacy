Privacy Policy for Solitaire Journey

Last updated: October 2, 2026

LandN Games Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Solitaire Journey. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Solitaire Journey does not require users to create an account and LandN Games Studio does not directly request personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address through the game.

The game uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • Device and advertising identifiers;
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad interactions;
  • Diagnostic and performance information;
  • Information related to advertisements displayed in the game.

Advertising

Solitaire Journey uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and interaction information for purposes such as displaying ads, measuring advertising performance, analytics, and preventing fraud and abuse.

Depending on your location and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Solitaire Journey uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent for advertising-related data processing.

Users in applicable regions may be presented with privacy choices regarding personalized advertising and the use of their information. Available privacy choices may also be changed through privacy options provided within the game where required.

Third-Party Services

Solitaire Journey uses services provided by Google, including Google AdMob and related Google advertising technologies.

Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

Local Game Data

Game progress, preferences, settings, scores, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on your device so that the game can save your progress and settings.

This locally stored game data is not used by LandN Games Studio to personally identify you.

Data Sharing

LandN Games Studio does not sell personal information. Information processed through advertising services may be transmitted to Google and its advertising technology partners as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention services.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the game. Data transmitted by third-party services such as Google AdMob is handled using the security measures provided by those services, including encryption in transit where applicable.

Data Retention and Deletion

LandN Games Studio does not maintain user accounts or a separate server-side database containing players' personal profiles for Solitaire Journey.

Locally stored game data remains on the user's device until it is removed through the game's functionality, application storage settings, or by uninstalling the game, depending on the data involved.

Information collected by third-party services such as Google is retained and deleted according to those providers' applicable retention policies.

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning data controlled by LandN Games Studio, users may contact us using the email address provided below.

Children's Privacy

Solitaire Journey is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the game.

Advertising and data processing may be subject to additional protections where required by applicable laws and Google Play policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
